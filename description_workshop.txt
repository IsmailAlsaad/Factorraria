Factorraria is a factory automation mod for terraria
inspired by Satisfactory, Dyson sphere program, and the Create mod from minecraft

It aims to incorporate factory building and resource management and automation to the terraria gameplay loop while re-imagining how the story and progression goes 

The mod is also designed to be played without an external wiki as it has knowledge based progression elements 

and it has dependency on the [b]"Ismail's Weapon reworks"[/b]
as at first the weapon reworks were supposed to be part of Factorraria
but later I decided to make it a separate mod if you would like to play with the weapon reworks alone without the factory automation aspect :) 

Both mods are currently in beta stage and cover only [b][i]pre-hm[/i][/b] content

reworked weapons list are in the description of the weapon reworks steampage
